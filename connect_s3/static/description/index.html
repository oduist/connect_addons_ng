Built for Connect

An extension of the Connect communication platform.

Recordings in your own bucket

Twilio writes each recording straight into your AWS S3 bucket, so you own the media from the moment the call ends.

One-button provisioning

Odoo creates the bucket, blocks public access and turns on encryption — and re-running it just re-applies the settings.

Least-privilege by default

The settings page writes the exact IAM policy for you, scoped to your bucket prefix and nothing else.

Credentials handled for you

Create or rotate the AWS credential Twilio uses without leaving Odoo, and copy the two values the Console asks for.

Retention on your terms

Set a retention window and a lifecycle rule expires the audio; the transcript and the summary stay in Odoo.

Playback and transcription, unchanged

The player, the download and OpenAI transcription all read from your bucket — nobody has to learn a new screen.

Switch over without a migration

Everything recorded before the switch keeps playing from Twilio. Turn it on today and only new calls move.
