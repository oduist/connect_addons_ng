Turn Twilio messages into CRM leads automatically!

The bridge that routes Twilio SMS and WhatsApp straight to your CRM pipeline.

This module is provided as a 30-day free trial. After the trial period ends, a purchase is required to continue using the application. For pricing and other details, visit https://oduist.com/pricing

Features

  • This is an extension module for the Connect application!
  • A lightweight glue module that connects Connect CRM with Connect Twilio.
  • Adds CRM Lead as a destination for inbound Twilio message routing.
  • Installs automatically once both Connect CRM and Connect Twilio are present — no extra setup.
