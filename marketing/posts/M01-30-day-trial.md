## Post

No demo request. No sales call. No "let's schedule 30 minutes to understand your needs." Install the module and it runs. ⏳

That's how every Oduist Connect module is licensed:

📆 Installing a module starts a **30-day trial for that module, automatically** — no registration required
🏭 During the trial the module is **fully functional in production**, so you evaluate it on real calls and real customers, not on a sandbox
🧩 The trial is **per module**: each one stamps its own install date and counts from there
🧪 Evaluation, development and staging use is free under the licence, trial or not
🔔 A systray banner shows the trial state and days remaining — and turns into a warning in the final 7 days
🛒 To keep a module in production afterwards you buy a commercial licence, from inside Odoo

A module whose install date can't be determined is treated as a full trial, never as an expired one. The default is in your favour.
