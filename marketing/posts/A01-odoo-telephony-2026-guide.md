## Post

There is no single "Odoo telephony" product. There are four paths, and they lead to very different places. 🧭

1️⃣ **Cloud CPaaS** — Twilio, Telnyx, Infobip, Vonage, Bird. A number, a browser phone, webhooks. Nothing to host.
2️⃣ **Self-hosted** — FreeSWITCH as a full PBX (Docker image, XML dialplan generated by Odoo, SIP firewall, local Piper TTS in 26 languages), or a LiveKit stack for WebRTC + SIP.
3️⃣ **Keep the PBX you have** — existing Asterisk / FreePBX / Issabel through a sidecar agent, or 3CX V20 through its server-side CRM integration.
4️⃣ **AI voice agents on top of any of them** — ElevenLabs, Telnyx AI Assistants, LiveKit Agents, Pipecat, Dograh.

**Oduist Connect** puts one technology-agnostic core underneath all four: the same calls, channels, recordings and messages, whichever road you take. Providers plug in as separate modules, and several can live in one database.

Which path is your company on right now? 👇
