## Post

Which telephony provider should you run with Odoo? Three questions settle it. ❓

**1. Do you want to run a server?**
No → cloud CPaaS: Twilio, Telnyx, Infobip, Vonage. A number, a browser phone, webhooks.
Yes → FreeSWITCH (Docker image, dialplan generated by Odoo, SIP firewall) or a self-hosted LiveKit stack.

**2. Do you already have a PBX?**
Then keep it. Asterisk / FreePBX / Issabel connect through a sidecar agent that holds the AMI link. 3CX V20 (PRO or AI edition) connects through its server-side CRM integration.

**3. Do you need messaging?**
SMS and WhatsApp: Twilio, Telnyx, Infobip, Bird. RCS today: Telnyx only.

Two honest caveats before you choose: Bird ships no browser phone — click-to-call rings your own mobile first. And 3CX reports calls to Odoo only after they end.

**Oduist Connect** speaks all of them against one call ledger, so the choice stays reversible.

Where would your team land? 👇
