# VoiceTel Browser Phone

The VoiceTel phone appears as a handset icon in the top bar of Odoo when
your administrator has enabled the browser phone on your Connect user.

## Calls

- **Dial** a number or an extension on the keypad, or use the **Contacts**
  and **Favorites** tabs.
- **Click-to-call** from any phone field: the call starts in the browser
  phone when it is registered. Otherwise the call is placed to your desk
  phone or VoiceML Phone app and the destination is connected when you
  answer.
- Incoming calls ring on every device registered with your SIP account at
  once: desk phone, VoiceML Phone app and this browser phone.

During a call: **mute**, **hold**, **DTMF** keypad, **transfer** (the other
party is handed over to the number you enter) and **hang up**.

## Settings

Your administrator sets your SIP account. Preferences on your Connect user
(**VoiceTel Phone** tab): ring volume, masking of phone numbers, call popup.

## Tips

- Keep one Odoo tab with the phone open; additional tabs share the same
  registration.
- Allow microphone access when the browser asks for it.
- After the computer wakes from sleep the phone re-registers; wait a few
  seconds before placing a call.
